AI-Based Decision Support Systems for Healthcare Diagnostics: A Decision Support Framework

Published in International Journal for Research in Applied Science & Engineering Technology (IJRASET), Vol. 13, Issue X, Oct 2025

This paper presents an AI-based decision support framework for healthcare diagnostics, integrating machine learning, deep learning, and explainable AI (XAI) techniques to provide real-time, interpretable insights for clinicians. The framework helps improve diagnostic accuracy, supports evidence-based decision-making, and bridges the gap between AI predictions and clinical usability.

Recommended citation: Taduri, L. (2025). "AI-Based Decision Support Systems for Healthcare Diagnostics: A Decision Support Framework." IJRASET, Vol. 13, Issue X, Oct 2025.
